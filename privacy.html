Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026.

This policy explains how Dankore Software LLC ("we") handles information on ethnicity-calculator.dankoresoft.com.

What you enter in the calculator

The ethnicities, percentages, and names you type are processed in your browser. We save them in your browser's local storage so your last calculation is there when you return. They are never sent to our servers. Use "Start over" or clear your browser data to remove them.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device, and pages viewed. It does not receive what you type into the calculator.

Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense to keep the calculator free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Payments and messages

If you buy a consultation, payment is handled by Stripe and we receive only what Stripe shares with merchants. If you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we use your message only to reply and provide the service.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
