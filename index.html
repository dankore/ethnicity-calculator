    How to Determine Your Parents' Ethnic Makeup

    To determine your parents' ethnic makeup, you can start by having a conversation with them about their ancestry and background. Ask questions about their parents and grandparents origin ethnic groups.

    Example:

    Let's say your father is 50% Fulani and 50% Hausa, and your mother is 25% Hausa, 25% Igala, and 50% Idoma. To calculate your ethnicity breakdown, you would input the following:

    • Father's Ethnicity 1: Fulani - 50%
    • Father's Ethnicity 2: Hausa - 50%
    • Mother's Ethnicity 2: Igala - 50%
    • Mother's Ethnicity 1: Hausa - 25%
    • Mother's Ethnicity 3: Idoma - 25%

    Based on this information, your ethnicity breakdown would be:

    • Hausa: 37.5%
    • Fulani: 25%
    • Igala: 25%
    • Idoma: 12.5%
